Izuba

A mobile wallet for Rwanda: one app for transfers to any network, shop payments, bills, savings and small loans, with every fee shown before you pay. This is a working prototype. Every franc in it is sandbox money.

  • Name check and scam warningsShows who you are paying and stops risky transfers.
  • Send to any network or bankFree inside Izuba. 1%, max RWF 500, to MoMo or Airtel.
  • Shop payments by till or QRNo fee for the customer.
  • Bills with instant tokensCash Power, water, TV, Irembo, RRA.
  • Split a billSend payment requests to friends and track who paid.
  • Scheduled paymentsRent, school fees and family support on autopilot.
  • Savings goals with interestLock money until a date. 6% a year.
  • Small loans and BoostClear limit, flat fee, overdraft when you're short.
  • Cash out with a one-time codeThe agent never handles your PIN.
  • Spending and fee insightsSee where money went and what fees cost you.

Names, numbers, merchants, fees and rates are invented for this demo. Demo PIN is 1234. A live version needs a National Bank of Rwanda licence or a licensed partner.
